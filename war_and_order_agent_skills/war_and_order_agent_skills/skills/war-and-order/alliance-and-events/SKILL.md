# Alliance and Events
Read event name, deadline, task conditions and reward from current screen; do not rely on remembered event calendars. A free claim or alliance-help action may be automated only when clearly identified and allowed by policy; verify counter/list/reward change. Require owner approval for messages, invitations, applications, alliance join/leave, diplomacy, war declarations, PvP/rallies with consequences, rare items and premium currency. Distinguish free claim from nearby purchase or exchange buttons. Never send social messages automatically.
