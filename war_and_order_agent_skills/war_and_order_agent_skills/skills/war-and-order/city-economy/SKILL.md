# City Economy
Confirm city screen. Read current resources, building name/level, prerequisites, price, duration and queue from the fresh frame. Prioritize owner goals and verified prerequisites; then sustainable economy and necessary military buildings. Do not upgrade randomly because a button is highlighted. Check available resources and preserve a reserve if the owner defined one. One upgrade at a time. If cost, queue or target level is unclear, do nothing. Stop at premium acceleration or purchase dialog. Verify the new queue/level/timer before updating memory. Report confirmed cost, reason and outcome.
