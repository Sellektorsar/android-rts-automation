# Hero and Army
Read only visible hero names, troop types/counts, levels, health and bonuses. Do not invent hidden stats or claim guaranteed victory. Before a march, verify target, target type, composition, march availability, cost/risk and expected timer. Require explicit owner approval for player PvP, risky rallies, rare-item consumption or irreversible upgrades. After dispatch, verify march and target on screen. If target ownership/type or troop information is unclear, stop at recommendation rather than dispatching.
