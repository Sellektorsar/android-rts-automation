# World Map and Monsters
Confirm world-map screen. After every pan/zoom, reacquire targets; old screen coordinates are invalid. Distinguish resource node, monster, player castle, alliance territory and unknown object. Before gathering, check target, free march, distance/time and composition if visible. For monsters, check available strength/risk indicators and avoid unlimited attack loops. Do not attack player-owned objects without explicit approval. After dispatch verify the march marker/list and destination. If cost, target class or risk is uncertain, do not send.
