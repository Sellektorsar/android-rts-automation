# Daily Planner
Refresh screen and state. Rank at most three candidate tasks by goal relevance, verified benefit, cost, queue availability, reversibility, troop/resource risk and perception confidence. Exclude tasks with unknown costs, ambiguous screens or missing approval. Choose one task, call its specialist skill, verify the outcome, update memory and replan. Stop on session action/time limit, repeated failure, unknown modal or owner-approval requirement. “Autonomous” means autonomy within configured permissions, not unlimited spending or risk. End with confirmed completed tasks, remaining work, observable state changes, uncertainties and next safe recommendation.
